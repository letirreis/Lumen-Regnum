Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to start?

No. The Explorer plan is free and asks for no card. When your campaign grows, you subscribe to Starter or Full.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You manage your subscription inside the app through the billing portal and can cancel at any time.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. You go back to the free plan and what already exists stays; you just cannot create beyond the plan limit.

Which RPG system does it work with?

Lumen Regnum focuses on story and table prep, not on the rules of a specific system. You describe your sheets however you like.

Do my players need an account?

Today the app is for the game master. A read-only link for players is on the way.

Is my data safe?

Each account only sees its own campaigns, and the limits of each plan are enforced on the server.
